Ceremonial Guidance
- Preparing for the Ceremony:
  - Set Your Intention — Before burning, take time to reflect on why you're making this offering.
  - Create Sacred Space — Light a candle, burn incense, or play ceremonial music.
  - Ground Yourself — Take three deep breaths. Feel your connection to the earth and the digital realm.
  - Write Your Intention — Craft a meaningful message that will be stored on-chain forever.
- The Burning Ritual:
  - Enter the Amount — Choose how many ETHO tokens you wish to offer.
  - Speak Your Intention — Type your message into the intention field.
  - Confirm the Transaction — Click "Burn Offering" and approve in your wallet.
  - Witness the Transformation — Tokens flow to the treasury and your Proof of Burn NFT is minted.
  - Receive Your NFT — The soulbound NFT appears in your wallet as eternal proof of your offering.
- After the Offering:
  - Sit in Gratitude — Feel the completion of the ceremony.
  - Journal Your Experience — Integration is key.
  - Share (Optional) — Share your experience with the community.
