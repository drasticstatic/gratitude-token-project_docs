How the AMM Works
1) Automated Market Maker — Trade tokens without order books using liquidity pools
2) Constant Product Formula — Prices are determined by the ratio of tokens in the pool (x * y = k)
3) Liquidity Providers — Earn fees by providing liquidity to pools
4) Low Slippage — Larger pools mean better prices and less price impact
5) Permissionless — Anyone can trade or provide liquidity 24/7
