At the heart of ACIM is the recognition that Knowledge is certain and requires no defense. Likewise, zero-knowledge shows that certainty can be shared without exposure.

> "Strictly speaking, words play no part at all in healing... Words are but symbols of symbols." — M-21.1:1,9

We translate this as: proofs over performance; witness over spectacle.

The altar is within. Our systems should respect that.
